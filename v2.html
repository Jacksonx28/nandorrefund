[ 03 · How it works ]

One journey.
No wallet until money moves.

01

Lock the milestone

A client starts a job from an X post or an email link, no wallet needed to begin. The milestone is locked in the escrow contract on X Layer, with the checklist and deadline written into the job.

02

Deliver, hash, accept

The freelancer delivers the work and its hash is recorded on-chain. The client signs acceptance, or doesn't. Silence is data too, and the circuit knows how to read it.

03

The circuit decides

The escrow contract feeds five facts to the referee circuit. The circuit answers, the contract pays. Nobody can lean on it, phone it, or rush it.

[ 04 · The verdicts ]

Three verdicts.
No discretion.

The referee reads five bits, in order: delivered · hash matches · checklist complete · deadline met · client accepted. Money arithmetic and the full hash check stay in the contract. The circuit decides.

Re-run the referee

Every verdict carries a replay. The exact inputs go back through the circuit, live, and the answer must match the one that moved the money. If we ever lied about an outcome, the replay would expose it in seconds. That is the whole trust model: don't trust us, re-run it.

First escrow free

Platforms take 10–20%, and dispute arbitration can add 5% more. Ours is 0.8% after a free first job, because the referee costs 0.0000022 OKB, not a support team's afternoon.
