[ How it works ]

One journey.
No wallet until money moves.

Three steps, and at no point does a human, including us, get to decide who keeps the money.

STEP 01

Lock the milestone

A client starts a job from an X post or an email link, no wallet needed to begin. The milestone is locked in the escrow contract on X Layer, with the checklist and deadline written into the job.

STEP 02

Deliver, hash, accept

The freelancer delivers the work and its hash is recorded on-chain. Job briefs and deliveries travel over TapeRelay, TapeOut's own relay, so the hash the escrow sees is the hash of what was actually delivered. The client signs acceptance, or doesn't. Silence is data too.

STEP 03

The circuit decides

The escrow contract feeds five facts to the referee circuit. The circuit answers, the contract pays. Nobody can lean on it, phone it, or rush it, and the verdict can be re-run by anyone.

[ The verdict table ]

Five bits in. One verdict out.

The referee reads five bits, in order: delivered · hash matches · checklist complete · deadline met · client accepted. Money arithmetic and the full 256-bit hash check stay in the contract, where they belong. The circuit decides the outcome.

[ Why this referee ]

Built so nobody has to trust us.

Re-run the referee

Every verdict carries a replay. The exact inputs go back through the circuit, live, and the answer must match the one that moved the money. If we ever lied about an outcome, the replay would expose it in seconds. That is the whole trust model: don't trust us, re-run it.

A split is a verdict, not a failure

Most escrow is binary: pay or refund, and every grey job becomes a fight. Work that arrived but was never accepted is the commonest dispute in freelance work, so the circuit has a preset answer for it: 50/50, decided in advance, argued by nobody.

First escrow free, then 0.8%

Platforms take 10–20%, and dispute arbitration can add 5% more. Ours is 0.8% after a free first job, because the referee costs 0.0000022 OKB, not a support team's afternoon.

No wallet until payout

Start a job, deliver to one, accept one, all from X or email. A wallet appears only at the moment money actually moves, which is the moment it should appear.
